How to play

  • Tap one of your pieces, then a glowing cell.
  • Green, next door: your piece buzzes over and leaves a copy behind.
  • Teal, two away: it flies there and leaves its old cell empty.
  • Every enemy touching the cell you land on switches to your side.
  • If a side can't move, the game ends and the other side takes every empty cell. Most cells wins.
  • Drag to turn and tilt the board, pinch to zoom. The ⊕ button straightens it again.
  • Tap during an animation to speed it up.
